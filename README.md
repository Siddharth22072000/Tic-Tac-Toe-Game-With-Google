What Was Built:
Game Modes & AI Engine:
Single Player vs AI: Featuring 3 difficulty tiers: Easy (casual), Medium (smart blocks & tactical play), and Master / Unbeatable (Minimax algorithm with alpha-beta heuristic evaluation).
2 Players (Pass & Play): Local multiplayer with customizable player names and real-time turn tracking.
Multiple Board Dimensions:
Classic 3 × 3 (3 in a row to win)
Expanded 4 × 4 (4 in a row to win)
Master 5 × 5 (4 in a row to win)
Gameplay Enhancements:
Move Hint: Calculates the optimal strategic move and highlights the cell with a glowing pulse.
Undo Move: Quickly reverse previous moves.
Celebration Effects: Animated winning lines, cell pulsing, and a full-screen victory confetti burst.
Audio & Haptics: Subtle sound effects and tactile vibration feedback on moves, wins, and draws (toggleable in Settings).
Persistent Statistics & History (Room Database):
Tracks total matches, player wins, draws, and win rates across sessions.
Match history sheet displaying board dimension, difficulty, move count, duration, and timestamp.
Individual match deletion and clear-all history options.
Visual Design & Themes:
4 themes: Cyber Neon, Galaxy Violet, Modern Light, and Retro Emerald.
Custom adaptive launcher icon and edge-to-edge Material 3 interface.
