# 🎮 Tic-Tac-Toe: AI Challenge

A feature-rich Tic-Tac-Toe game with multiple board sizes, intelligent AI opponents, local multiplayer, customizable themes, animated effects, and persistent match statistics.

Challenge yourself against an AI with three difficulty levels, compete with a friend, and track your performance across every match.

## ✨ Features

### 🤖 Game Modes & AI Engine

- **Single Player vs AI:** Play against an intelligent computer opponent with three difficulty levels:
  - 🟢 **Easy:** Casual gameplay for beginners.
  - 🟡 **Medium:** Smart blocking and tactical move selection.
  - 🔴 **Master / Unbeatable:** Advanced AI powered by the Minimax algorithm with alpha-beta pruning and heuristic evaluation.
- **2 Players — Pass & Play:** Enjoy local multiplayer on the same device.
- **Custom Player Names:** Personalize the names of both players.
- **Real-Time Turn Tracking:** Clearly indicates whose turn it is throughout the match.

### 📐 Multiple Board Dimensions

Choose the board size that matches your preferred challenge.

| Board Mode | Grid Size | Winning Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Classic | 3 × 3 | Connect 3 in a row |
| Expanded | 4 × 4 | Connect 4 in a row |
| Master | 5 × 5 | Connect 4 in a row |

### 🕹️ Enhanced Gameplay

- **Move Hint:** Calculates a strategic move and highlights the recommended cell with a glowing pulse animation.
- **Undo Move:** Reverse previous moves and reconsider your strategy.
- **Winning Animations:** Animated winning lines and pulsing cells.
- **Victory Confetti:** Full-screen confetti effects when a player wins.
- **Draw Detection:** Recognizes drawn matches and provides appropriate feedback.
